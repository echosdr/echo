# Privacy Policy for Echo

**Last Updated:** September 14, 2026

I (Mark Garrison Jr.) built Echo to listen to radio signals, not to listen to you. Here is exactly how your data is handled.

## 1. No Tracking, No Ads, No Analytics
I do not know who you are, how you use the app, or what stations you listen to.
- **No Third-Party Analytics:** Echo does not contain Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or any equivalent analytics platform.
- **No Ads:** Echo has no advertising or advertising SDKs. It does not track you across apps or build an advertising profile about you.

## 2. Your Data is Yours
Echo stores your data on your device.
- **Echo Pro and iCloud:** Echo Pro can sync supported recordings, transcripts, translations, favorites, saved stations, saved servers, notes, and preferences through your private iCloud account.
- Your Echo data is stored on your device and, when synced with Echo Pro, in your private iCloud/CloudKit database.

## 3. Recordings, Transcription & Translation
Recording, transcription, and translation are handled on your device.
- **On-Device Processing:** Transcription uses on-device speech recognition models, and translation uses Apple's on-device frameworks.
- Your recordings, radio audio, transcripts, translations, notes, and favorites are not sent to Hugging Face.

## 4. Connections to Radio Servers
When you connect to a KiwiSDR, OpenWebRX, WebSDR, or FM-DX Webserver, your device makes a direct connection to that independently operated server.
- **IP Addresses:** The server operator may see your public IP address and ordinary connection information in its logs.
- **Independent Servers:** Echo does not control these servers or their logging practices. It sends only what is needed to make and use the radio connection you requested.

## 5. Speech Model Downloads
Echo downloads the speech recognition models used for transcription from Hugging Face.
- Like any internet service, Hugging Face may receive your IP address and ordinary technical information about the download. Its handling of that information is governed by its own privacy practices.
- Hugging Face provides the model files. Your radio audio is processed on your device and is never sent to Hugging Face.

## 6. Location Data
Echo may ask for your location to show you the distance to radio servers.
- **On-Device Only:** Your location is used on your device to calculate that distance. Echo does not upload or share your precise location.

## 7. Deleting Your Data
Deleting Echo removes the app's local data from that device.
- **iCloud Sync:** Content already synced with Echo Pro may remain in your iCloud account. When you delete synced content inside Echo, that deletion is also reflected across your synced devices through iCloud.
